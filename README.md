# Project (IP3_2D_CF) 2D SuperHero Game

This project is a 2D Flying game which utilizes the technology LeapMotion to move the in-game character. 
The latest version of this respository supports mouse controls only. Older versions will most likely not work with LeapMotion unless updated.

About

This was university project. My team and I were tasked with creating a game for children and 'simulating' an augmented reality (AR) experience within a car setting. We decided to create a flying superhero
game where the character soars through the sky collecting coins and power-ups whilst evading the evil villain attacks and trying to take him down. 

How to Play?

Controls:

LeapMotion (https://www.ultraleap.com/product/leap-motion-controller/) - A device that tracks your hands movement and gestures.

The control scheme for LeapMotion was kept simple given our target audience (children) for the project. The pitch of your hand was tracked, and based on the angle of your hand that would determine the angle
of the character in-game. By closing your fist you could make the player shoot. It's recommended that you remove any jewellery and or any long sleeved clothing to avoid running into any problems with 
LeapMotion detecting your hand.

Mouse controls:

By moving the mouse the player's character, the game will calculate the angle and the player will follow.  Left clicking the mouse will make the player shoot, and by holding the left-click this will make the player
continously fire until lifted.
